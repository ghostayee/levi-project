The I-S&S-1 is a compact, highly efficient rotor-based energy generation system designed for small-scale hydroelectric applications. It enables clean energy production in low-flow environments where traditional turbines are inefficient or impractical.

  • Operating Principle: Advanced rotor geometry optimized for low water head and variable flow rates
  • Installation Flexibility: Rivers, irrigation canals, streams, fish ladders, existing infrastructure
