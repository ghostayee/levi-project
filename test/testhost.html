ITEG – Business Plan

The Overview

ITEG is a proprietary rotor-based energy technology developed by JWTeam under the JWT EUWEB 2030 framework. It delivers high-efficiency energy conversion in low-velocity and low-head flow conditions — environments where traditional turbines perform poorly. ITEG enables decentralized, sustainable, and economically viable clean energy production from water and air flows, while integrating seamlessly with other JWT green technologies such as GSMF, MHLM, and PCRR resilience solutions.

Core Innovation

  • Advanced rotor geometry optimized through fluid dynamics research
  • Superior performance in low flow speed / low head conditions
  • Fish-friendly and low environmental impact design
  • Modular, scalable, and easy-to-deploy architecture
  • Integration-ready with smart grids, IoT, and storage

Strategic Objectives

  • Accelerate ecological transition with affordable clean energy
  • Enable energy recovery from industrial & municipal flows
  • Support territorial development via Dream.ZONE projects
  • Create new economic value using JWT NFW/NFT model
  • Contribute to EU Green Deal, PNRR Missions, and UN SDGs

Target Markets & Applications

Micro & Pico Hydro

Rural electrification, remote communities, irrigation canals

Industrial Energy Recovery

Factories, wastewater plants, cooling systems

Agriculture & Agro-Energy

Smart irrigation systems with on-site power

River & Marine Energy

Low-impact hydrokinetic installations

Urban & Smart Cities

Building-integrated and peri-urban energy solutions

Large Territorial Projects

Dream.ZONE regional energy platforms

Monetization Model (NFW/NFT Rights)

  • L (License): Right to produce and distribute
  • S (Sales Rights): Autonomy to sell ITEG systems
  • I (Income Investment): Participation in revenue stream
  • J (Joint Venture): Strategic partnership with control option (30/21 model)

All transactions benefit from substantial tax incentives (50% tax credit + 50% corporate tax reduction), making acquisition close to “ZERO net cost” for qualified operators.

Competitive Advantages

  • Lower installation and civil works costs
  • Minimal environmental footprint
  • Synergy with other JWT technologies
  • Integration into Dream.ZONE ecosystem

Sustainability & Impact

  • Reduction of CO₂ emissions
  • Energy independence for local communities
  • Circular economy in water & industrial sectors
  • Creation of green jobs in manufacturing, installation, and maintenance
