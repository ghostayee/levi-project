ITEG Rotore

Flagship Commercial Rotor Technology

Official Commercial Name

ITEG Rotore is the flagship commercial product line of the ITEG technology. It is a next-generation, high-efficiency rotor system engineered to generate clean energy from low-velocity water and air flows — environments where traditional turbines deliver poor or uneconomical performance.

Flagship Product

Turning natural and industrial flows into clean, reliable energy.

⚡ Key Features

  • Advanced proprietary rotor geometry optimized for low head and low speed
  • High efficiency even with variable or turbulent flows
  • Fish-friendly design with minimal environmental impact
  • Modular and scalable architecture (Pico to industrial scale)
  • Robust composite materials (CMTC)
  • Low maintenance and long operational lifespan
  • Easy integration with smart monitoring and storage
