Legal Titles – PCRR Framework

The Legal Title forms the official legal foundation for each technology module within the PCRR framework. It refers to formal registrations, authorizations, and intellectual property protections granted by competent authorities that establish ownership and exclusive rights over innovations. These Legal Titles are essential for credibility, investor confidence, regulatory compliance, and secure commercialization.

Supporting Legal Framework

  • Official registrations with the Italian Ministry of Economic Development (MISE)
  • Industrial Property registrations at UIBM (Italian Patent and Trademark Office)
  • Alignment with EU Green Deal and PNRR regulations
  • Preparation for international extensions through WIPO, EPO, and national patent offices
  • Integration with NFT GREEN licensing model for transparent rights management

Strategic Importance

The Legal Title not only protects the core inventions but also enables safe technology transfer, licensing agreements, and international expansion while maintaining full control and traceability. By securing strong Legal Titles, PCRR ensures that every module is fully compliant, bankable, and ready for large-scale deployment across different territories and jurisdictions.
