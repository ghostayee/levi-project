Intangible Heritage – ESCP_IP (ITEG)

Official Title

ESCP_IP – ITEG represents the complete Intangible Heritage of the ITEG technology. It is the strategic intellectual property and commercialization framework that protects, organizes, and valorizes all intangible assets related to the ITEG Rotore system invented by Pelizza.

Protected Innovation

Transforming inventions into secure, monetizable assets.

Scope of ESCP_IP – ITEG

  • Core Patent Portfolio (MISE-ITEG – Pelizza)
  • European & International Patent Strategy (EPO + WIPO)
  • Industrial Designs for rotor geometries
  • Trademarks for “ITEG Rotore” brand
  • Know-How & Trade Secrets
  • Technology Integration Protocols (GSMF, MHLM)
  • Commercialization Rights Framework (NFW/NFT)

Strategic Objectives

  • Protect innovations at international standards
  • Create legal foundation for global commercialization
  • Enable secure issuance of NFW/NFT rights
  • Facilitate technology transfer & partnerships
  • Maximize economic value of intangible assets
  • Support Dream.ZONE territorial projects

Key Components

Patents+ – HWFC, CMTC, SDNA, MHLM Integration
Legal Titles – MISE-ITEG (Pelizza)
Commercial Framework – NFW/NFT model
Brand Protection – ITEG Rotore trademark
Knowledge Assets – Documentation & R&D data

Strategic Importance

ESCP_IP – ITEG is the central pillar of the intangible heritage of the ITEG project. It transforms Pelizza’s technical inventions into a protected, monetizable, and scalable asset within the JWT EUWEB 2030 ecosystem. This framework ensures long-term control, legal security, and maximum value extraction from innovation while supporting the ecological transition across Europe and beyond.
