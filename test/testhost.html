Pelizza: Inventor of ITEG Rotor Technology

Publication / Thesis

Abstract

Pelizza is the principal inventor of the ITEG (Integrated Turbine Energy Generator) system — a groundbreaking rotor technology designed for highly efficient energy extraction from low-velocity water and air flows. His innovations address one of the most persistent challenges in renewable energy: achieving viable performance in real-world, low-head, and variable flow conditions.
