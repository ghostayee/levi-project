MISE‑ITEG (Pelizza)

Official Title

MISE‑ITEG (Pelizza) is the official legal and industrial property title protecting the ITEG technology, developed under the JWT / JWTeam framework. It represents formal recognition and protection of the innovative rotor technology by the Italian Ministry of Economic Development (MISE).

Legal Anchor

Securing exclusive rights for production, commercialization, and licensing.

Scope of Protection

  • Advanced rotor geometry & fluid dynamics profiles
  • Proprietary blade design & flow optimization
  • Manufacturing processes & material specifications
  • System integration methodologies
  • Control & efficiency optimization algorithms

Legal Status

  • Registered with MISE (Ministero dello Sviluppo Economico)
  • Inventor: Pelizza
  • Owner / Applicant: JWT – Joules Water Team / JWTeam
  • European & International extension in process (EPO + WIPO)

Strategic Importance

MISE‑ITEG (Pelizza) constitutes the foundational legal pillar for the entire ITEG commercialization program. It secures exclusive rights to produce, license, and monetize the technology through the JWT NFW/NFT model.

  • Safe technology transfer and licensing
  • Issuance of tokenized rights (NFW)
  • Strong protection in Italy, Europe, and international markets
  • High-value positioning in Dream.ZONE territorial projects
