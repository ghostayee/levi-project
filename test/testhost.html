ITEG Patents+ Portfolio

Overview

The ITEG Patents+ portfolio represents the complete set of protected intellectual property and technological innovations developed around the ITEG rotor system by inventor Pelizza. This portfolio forms the technical backbone of the ITEG technology and provides strong competitive protection for commercialization through the JWT NFW/NFT model.

1. HWFC – High Water Flow Capture

Advanced rotor geometry and flow management system designed to maximize energy extraction from low-velocity and turbulent water flows.

2. CMTC – Composite Materials Turbine Components

Proprietary composite materials and manufacturing processes offering superior strength, corrosion resistance, lightweight construction, and extended durability.

3. MHLM Integration

Innovative protocols between ITEG rotors and MHLM mixing technology for combined energy generation and fluid treatment applications.

4. SDNA – Smart Dynamic Neural Adaptation

AI-powered intelligent control system that dynamically optimizes rotor performance in real-time, enabling predictive maintenance and maximum efficiency.
