Drop-in modular SDGC stack — pre-engineered for rapid deployment in coastal, island and arid environments.

♻️ The SDGC Cycle

A closed thermal loop where geothermal energy does the work usually done by electrical pumps.

1
Heat Capture

Shallow geothermal probe (80–150 m) extracts low-enthalpy heat.

2
Vacuum Evaporation

Saline feed flashes into vapor under controlled vacuum at 50 °C.

3
Condensation

Pure water condenses, releasing heat back into the loop.

4
Re-mineralization

Bio-mineral cartridge tunes water to potable spec.

Thermal range across the cycle (cold ↔ hot):

15 °C condenser50 °C evaporator95 °C heat recovery

💰 Pricing & Economics

SDGC delivers desalinated water at 0.48 €/m³ on average — competitive with surface-water utilities and dramatically below reverse-osmosis on islands.
