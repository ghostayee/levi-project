Overview

Joules, Water and renewable primary resources

JWT is the operating company behind a set of industrial green patents designed to bring energy, water and food autonomy to local communities. The model is distributed, pervasive and aligned with the ecological transition goals of the EU.

Energy

Distributed energy solutions for local production, efficiency and resilience.

Water

Desalination, treatment and integrated water management for sustainable territories.

Circular Economy

Turning waste, biomass and local resources into value with compact industrial systems.

Safety

JWT Safety

JWT adopts a culture of prevention inspired by AIAS, the Italian association founded in 1975, to spread safety and health in all working and living environments. The approach combines occupational health, environmental protection, risk management and sustainability across all our technologies and projects.

We support integrated safety, training, and continuous improvement so that every project is designed and delivered with traceable protection standards and a strong commitment to human wellbeing.

Security

JWT Security

Security at JWT covers privacy, data protection, cybersecurity and the safeguard of intellectual property. We work with the principles of GDPR, DPIA, and secure smart-working practices to protect data, stakeholders and digital infrastructure.

We also apply a governance approach for sensitive information, contractual compliance and secure collaboration with partners, authorities and industrial clients.

Team

About the team

JWT is a multidisciplinary group of professionals who combine research, engineering, industrial design, environmental science and operations. The team is focused on turning innovation into useful systems for communities and industrial users.

The core team includes Vito Lavanga, Simona Ramponi, Rita Ferro, Alessandra Trinca, Stefano Farnè, Paolo Broglio and other specialists supporting research, implementation and stakeholder engagement.

Applied innovation

Energy systems, microalgae, biogas, desalination and water management are developed with pilot-ready engineering and industrial validation.

Territorial deployment

The business model supports local autonomy, SMEs, public initiatives and industrial circularity in a distributed format.

From patents to impact

Each patent is designed to reduce dependency on fossil inputs, improve water resilience and create circular uses of biomass, waste and local resources.

Vision

Health, safety and inclusivity

JWT’s vision is to employ the technologies available today to guarantee energy, water and every essential resource, without compromising the nature and health of the planet.

The mission is to leave future generations a safer, healthier and more inclusive environment while enabling a new model of local autonomy and sustainability.

Mission

Respect, security and economic value

JWT promotes the use, diffusion and exploitation of its patents to respond to the demand for energy, water and other resources with maximum efficiency and respect for the environment.

The company builds social and economic resilience through sustainable innovation, territorial partnerships and high-value industrial solutions that can be adopted by SMEs and local communities.
